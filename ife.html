Travel & Accommodation

Getting into Albania

We recommend flying into Tirana International Airport.

Getting around

  • 25-minute radius. Our wedding is in the capital, Tirana. The venue is within 25 minutes of both the city centre and the airport.
  • Rides. Rent a car (e.g. Rent Motors, beside the airport) or use a local taxi app: VrapOn Taxi Albania, Patoko or Speed Taxi Albania. Uber and Bolt are not available in Albania.
  • Local transport. Albania has reliable buses, but most require cash in Albanian Lek. Exchange at the airport to avoid surge charges at ATMs.
  • Spending & currency. The national currency is the Albanian Lek, which can only be exchanged in Albania (airports). Euros are also widely used. Bring some cash for the city centre and local markets; international bank cards work for other expenses.
